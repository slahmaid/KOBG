# KOBG Great Leads variants (Pass 7) — internal / ops

Not a public SEO landing. Exact French variants for ads/tests. Homepage visible lead stays the approved Promise + Problem-Solution sentence. Frameworks are heuristics — **not** conversion guarantees.

**Awareness match (homepage first ~50 words):** Solution → Product-aware hypothesis — visitor already wants Rival gloves / boxing gear; hero channels choice (sac vs sparring) rather than mass desire or #1 claims. Badge « Distributeur officiel » and promo chrome remain secondary (existing claims, not newly asserted in the lead).

## Big Idea

**Status:** documented-only (truthful operational framing; not placed on homepage)

**Placement:** Future ads / email; not homepage (would compete with approved lead).

**Variant (Français):**
« Un parcours simple au Maroc : choisissez sac ou sparring, commandez en ligne, payez à la livraison. »
